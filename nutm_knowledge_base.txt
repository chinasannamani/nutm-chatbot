### About NUTM
keywords: what is nutm, nigerian university of technology and management, overview, university
The Nigerian University of Technology and Management (NUTM) is a tertiary education institution in Nigeria, established to deliver world-class Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics (STEM) and Management education at undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral levels. It is a pioneering initiative to identify and train the next generation of leaders in technology and management for Africa. Its tagline is "In Africa, for Africa and the World."

### NUTM vision, mission and values
keywords: vision, mission, values, goals, ranking, 2030, aspiration
Vision: to be ranked among the top 50 global institutions focused on technology and management. NUTM aims to reach 10,000 physical students and 500,000 digital students by 2030, and in the next 10 years aspires to be among the top five universities in Africa and the best in Nigeria.
Mission: to develop innovative leaders in technology and management who will create an impact in Africa and the world.
Values: Integrity, Excellence, Collaboration, Sustainability, Innovation.

### The NUTM Edge (what makes NUTM different)
keywords: edge, features, benefits, why choose nutm, tedlab, shadow a leader, mentorship, immersion, career center
The NUTM Edge includes: Global Faculty (lectures and workshops by leading faculty from Africa and beyond); Career Development (a Career Center with placement support and a workplace-ready toolkit); TEDLab (an entrepreneurship capstone project to build a venture from scratch); Shadow a Leader (a six-week internship, full-time or part-time, shadowing a senior leader and working on a company project); Industry Mentorship (guest lectures, workshops and individual mentorship by eminent leaders and entrepreneurs); and Immersion Programs (exposure to international business, digitization and culture).

### NUTM accreditation and approval
keywords: accredited, nuc, national universities commission, approved, fec, federal executive council, recognized, legitimate
NUTM has approval from the Federal Executive Council (FEC) to operate as a university in Nigeria and is accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC) to offer full bachelor's degree programmes. NUTM offers its postgraduate master's programmes (MBA, Software Engineering, Data Science) with FEC approval and NUC accreditation. NUTM is also profiled on the JAMB portal and at JAMB-accredited CBT centres.

### Postgraduate programmes at NUTM (overview)
keywords: postgraduate, masters, master's, msc, pg, graduate programs, mba, software engineering, data science
NUTM offers three master's programmes: the MBA in Leadership, Technology & Entrepreneurship, the MSc in Software Engineering, and the MSc in Data Science. All are available full-time (18 months, 3 semesters, on-campus Monday to Friday) and part-time (24 months, 4 semesters, online on Fridays 1 PM to 6 PM WAT and Saturdays 9 AM to 6 PM WAT, with an on-campus immersive week each semester). Tuition: MSc Data Science N5,000,000; MSc Software Engineering N5,000,000; MBA N8,000,000. There is no application fee. Postgraduate admissions email: pg.admissions@nutm.edu.ng.

### Postgraduate application process and admission stages (all master's programmes)
keywords: how to apply, application, admission process, stages, steps, assessment, interview, apply postgraduate, apply masters, selection
Postgraduate admission has four stages: (1) Online application - complete the online form at app.nutm.edu.ng; there is NO application fee. (2) Online assessment - shortlisted candidates take an assessment of verbal, quantitative and critical thinking abilities (about 60-90 minutes, completed within a defined window); applicants with a valid GMAT/GRE score are eligible for a waiver. (3) Telephone interview - a 20-minute call evaluating your aspirations and fit for the programme. (4) Panel interview - a 30-minute interaction with a panel assessing aptitude, leadership potential and motivation. Admissions are conducted in rounds, so early applications are encouraged.

### Postgraduate FAQ: Do I need GMAT or GRE?
keywords: gmat, gre, test, entrance exam, waiver, admission test
No. NUTM conducts its own admissions assessment test, which applicants complete. Applicants who do not have a GMAT or GRE score take the NUTM Admissions Assessment Test. Applicants with a valid GMAT/GRE score are eligible for a waiver from the online assessment. (The MBA brochure also advises that if you think your qualifications exempt you, you should consult the admissions team.)

### Postgraduate FAQ: Are part-time students required to attend campus? Immersive weeks
keywords: immersive week, attend campus, part-time, on campus, compulsory, mandatory, visit lagos
Yes. Immersive weeks are mandatory for part-time students. Part-time students study online but visit the NUTM Lagos campus once per semester for an Immersive Week of workshops, masterclasses with industry experts, hands-on projects and networking. International participants may be allowed to join the immersive week remotely, but Nigerian students are strongly recommended to attend in person. Full-time students attend classes on campus regularly and do not have separate immersive weeks.

### Postgraduate FAQ: Can I switch between full-time and part-time?

No. Students must select their preferred format (full-time or part-time) at the time of admission.

keywords: meals, food, refectory, allowance, travel cost, transport, feeding, upkeep
Students pay for their own meals; a refectory is available on campus. Meals, travel and allowances are not provided by NUTM, and you must fund your own travel to and from the university. Fees cover only tuition and accommodation, and students purchase their own course materials.

### Undergraduate FAQ: Campus location and off-campus living
keywords: campus, location, where, hostel, accommodation, off-campus, live outside, own apartment, residence
The campus is at 6 Freetown Road, Apapa, Lagos. You can live in your own off-campus apartment but must promptly attend all academic and mandatory extracurricular activities.

### Undergraduate FAQ: Transfers and direct entry
keywords: transfer, direct entry, de, from another university, 200 level, change university, level
You can transfer to NUTM from another university, but not into 100 Level. NUTM began offering undergraduate programmes in 2023, and transfers into other levels began in 2024. Direct entry is available, but not into 100 Level.

### Undergraduate FAQ: Programme length

The minimum duration of the computing programmes (B.Sc. Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Information Technology) is four (4) academic sessions, or eight (8) consecutive semesters, for candidates who enter through UTME.
